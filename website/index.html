01 / RESEARCH DIRECTION

Better reasoning is
a systems problem.

We are interested in the mechanisms and methods behind reliable problem-solving: how a model structures work, when it should spend more inference-time computation, and how the result can be tested rather than simply trusted.

01
⌘

Structured & hierarchical reasoning

Explore multi-level computation, problem decomposition, and architectures that build intermediate representations instead of handling every challenge in one pass.

HRM / DECOMPOSITION
02
◷

Inference-time computation

Study when systems should pause, plan, search, verify, or allocate additional compute — and how those choices affect latency, cost, and solution quality.

SYSTEM 2-INSPIRED
03
⌁

Evaluation & reproducibility

Build workflows that make claims traceable: explicit baselines, useful metrics, controlled experiments, ablations, and transparent limitations.

MEASURE WHAT MATTERS

↳ These are active areas of exploration, not claims of validated breakthroughs. Results will be presented with their methods, baselines, and limitations.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Confidence is not
evidence.

Research tooling should make it easier to inspect what happened, understand what remains uncertain, and reproduce the work without hiding behind polished output.

01

Evidence before rhetoric

Connect substantive claims to sources, data, experiments, or clearly stated assumptions.

↗
02

Reproducibility by design

Record configurations, data provenance, search decisions, baselines, and evaluation methods.

↗
03

Human accountability

AI assistance does not replace scientific judgment, independent validation, or responsible review.

↗
04

Honest boundaries

Separate working prototypes, exploratory experiments, and demonstrated results. Make limitations visible.
