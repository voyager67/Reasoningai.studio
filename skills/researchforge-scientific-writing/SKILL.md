# Scientific Writing

## Purpose

Help authors communicate original work clearly while preserving scientific meaning, uncertainty, and their intellectual ownership.

## Input

Bring any material already available: research question, outline, methods, results, references, target audience/venue, author voice, and any word limit. If an essential item is absent, state the gap and proceed with a clearly labelled provisional plan rather than repeatedly asking for information that is already present.

## Workflow

1. Build a manuscript spine: problem; gap; question; contribution; method; evidence; implications; limitations.
2. Create a claim-evidence map before drafting. Mark unsupported claims as placeholders rather than filling them with invented facts.
3. Draft each section for its function: abstract (question/method/result/meaning); introduction (problem/gap/contribution); methods (reproducibility); results (observations); discussion (interpretation and limits).
4. Use precise nouns and verbs, stable definitions, consistent units, and restrained claims. Define acronyms at first use.
5. Separate measured result, interpretation, speculation, and future work. Use citations to support specific claims, not as decoration.
6. Revise in passes: structure, logic, evidence, terminology, concision, then copyediting.
7. Compare each table/figure against manuscript text and ensure all cited sources exist and are relevant.
8. Preserve author voice and flag any substantive change in meaning for author approval.

## Required output

A revised section or manuscript, a change summary, unresolved evidence/citation questions, and a list of author decisions still required.

## Integrity and safety gates

- Never invent data, methods, citations, quotes, or author contributions.
- Do not disguise AI-generated text as verified source material or fabricate authorship statements.
- Do not imply causal or generalization evidence that the design does not provide.
- Do not remove relevant limitations or negative results for rhetorical convenience.
