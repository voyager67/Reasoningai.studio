# Thesis Roadmap

## Purpose

Help a student make steady progress while preserving a coherent question and realistic research scope.

## Input

Bring any material already available: degree requirements, topic, supervisor feedback, time horizon, methods, data access, ethics requirements, and current work. If an essential item is absent, state the gap and proceed with a clearly labelled provisional plan rather than repeatedly asking for information that is already present.

## Workflow

1. Extract formal requirements: format, word limits, deadlines, review gates, and submission rules.
2. Define the central question, contribution, population/system, method, and boundary of the thesis.
3. Map chapters to argumentative roles and show how each chapter contributes to answering the central question.
4. Build a literature plan with search, screening, reading, synthesis, and update milestones.
5. Define methods, data, evaluation, ethics, analysis, and reproducibility deliverables before drafting results.
6. Create milestones with dependencies, acceptance criteria, supervisor review points, and contingency paths.
7. Identify scope risks and predefine what can be cut without invalidating the thesis.
8. Set a recurring evidence review: does current work still answer the central question?

## Required output

Thesis charter; chapter map; literature plan; methods and evaluation plan; milestone table; risk register; and submission readiness criteria.

## Integrity and safety gates

- Do not promise a result or publication acceptance.
- Do not treat a schedule as a substitute for supervisor or institutional requirements.
- Do not include confidential participant or institutional data in public thesis planning artifacts.
- Keep the scope feasible for the actual time, methods, and resources.
